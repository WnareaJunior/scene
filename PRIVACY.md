# SCENE: Local Events Map — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 20, 2026**

SCENE ("scene", "we") is a map-based party and event discovery app. This policy
describes what we collect, why, and what we do with it.

## What we collect

- **Account information.** Your email address, a username, a password (stored
  only as a salted hash), and optionally a bio, gender, interests, and a
  profile photo.
- **Location.** With your permission, your device's location is used to center
  the map, find parties near you, and set the location of parties you post.
  Location is used at the time of the request; we do not run background
  location tracking and we do not store a history of your movements.
- **Content you post.** Parties you create (title, description, address,
  time, photo, tags, capacity), your RSVPs, and who you follow.
- **Photos.** Only the photos you explicitly choose to upload (a party flyer
  or your profile picture). We never access your photo library beyond the
  picker you use to select them.

## What we use it for

- Showing parties near you, on the map and in the feed.
- Letting you post parties, RSVP, and follow hosts.
- Signing you in and keeping your account secure.

That's the whole list. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with data
brokers, and we do not use it for advertising. There are no third-party
analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

## What other users can see

Your username, profile photo, bio, interests, follower/following counts, the
public parties you host, and (where the host has attendee visibility on) your
RSVP to a party. Your email address is never shown to other users.

## Service providers

Your data is stored with the infrastructure providers that run scene: our API
runs on Render, our database is hosted by Supabase (PostgreSQL), and uploaded
images are stored on AWS S3. Address search and geocoding on the party-posting
screen use the Google Maps Platform, which receives the addresses or
coordinates you look up.

## Data retention & deletion

Your account and content remain until you delete them. You can take down any
party you posted from inside the app. To delete your account and all
associated data, email us at the address below and we will remove it within
30 days.

## Children

scene is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to
create an account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
